What we do

Infrastructure work, without the guesswork

Most outages aren't caused by exotic failures — they're caused by systems nobody fully understands anymore. We map what's actually running, fix what's fragile, and leave your team with a system they can operate without us.

We work embedded with your engineers for the length of an engagement, then step back. No retainer required to keep the lights on.
